Research Opportunities

I am recruiting undergraduate researchers to join ongoing projects in the Tangible Media Group at the MIT Media Lab, advised by Prof. Hiroshi Ishii.

I welcome curious and motivated students from diverse backgrounds. Prior research experience is not required.

Currently Recruiting: IAP 2027 · Spring 2027 · Summer 2027

Open Positions

01. dear___: Interactive Memory and TeleAbsence

Areas: HCI, Psychology, Cognitive Science, Social Sciences, Interaction Design, Software Development

Location: Remote or in-person, subject to MIT policies

This project explores how digital interfaces shape the ways people remember and connect with absent loved ones. Building on dear___, an interactive memory application presented at UIST 2026, we investigate new ways to interact with emotionally meaningful memories.

Possible contributions include:

  • Literature reviews on memory, emotion, and human relationships
  • User studies, interviews, and qualitative analysis
  • Interaction design and digital prototyping
  • iOS application development

02. Tangible Music Co-Ideation

Areas: Computer Science, Generative AI, Music Technology, Interaction Design, Mechanical Engineering, Physical Computing

Location: Primarily in-person at the MIT Media Lab

This project explores collaborative music creation with generative AI through tangible interfaces. We combine physical objects, sensors, MIDI instruments, and real-time music generation models to develop new forms of musical interaction.

Possible contributions include:

  • Generative AI and machine learning integration
  • Real-time audio and software development
  • Hardware design, electronics, and prototyping
  • Musical interaction design and evaluation

Experience in at least one relevant area is preferred. Expertise in all areas is not required.

Eligibility & Commitment

Who can apply?

  • MIT undergraduate students from all majors and class years
  • Wellesley College undergraduates who meet MIT UROP cross-registration requirements
  • Eligible MIT undergraduate exchange students

Time commitment

  • Approximately 5–10 hours per week during the academic year
  • Minimum one full semester (approximately 4 months)
  • Longer commitments are strongly preferred
  • Students beginning in IAP are encouraged to continue through Spring

Compensation

UROP opportunities may be completed for hourly pay or academic credit, depending on funding availability and individual arrangements. Students cannot receive both pay and credit for the same project in the same term.

Wellesley students must cross-register each term and are not eligible for UROP Office Direct Funding. Wellesley IAP UROPs are paid only.

How to Apply

Please email bobwei@media.mit.edu with:

  1. A brief introduction and which project interests you
  2. Your CV, portfolio, or GitHub (if available)
  3. Your relevant skills and what you would like to contribute
  4. Your availability, including your preferred starting term, hours per week, and expected duration

Subject: [UROP] Project Name – Your Name

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Early inquiries are encouraged, particularly for students seeking paid UROPs.

After Acceptance

Once we agree to work together, you will need to:

  1. Develop a research project plan with your supervisor.
  2. Decide whether to participate for pay or academic credit.
  3. Submit your formal application through the MIT UROP system before the relevant deadline.
  4. Complete any required registration, location forms, and other onboarding requirements.

Formal UROP applications are required every term, including when continuing the same project.
