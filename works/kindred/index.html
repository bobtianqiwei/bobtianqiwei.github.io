Kindred

A private iPhone journal for relationships, intimate moments, and reflection over time.

Bob Tianqi Wei
Independent Product · Product Design · iOS Development · 2026

Kindred helps people preserve meaningful experiences with the context needed to revisit them later. Each Moment combines personal writing with people, activities, time, place, feelings, photos, and optional intimacy and Apple Health details.

Download on the App Store
The problem

Context disappears

General-purpose journals preserve a story, but people, activities, places, and feelings remain buried in unstructured notes.

Memories stay disconnected

Photos, calendar events, messages, and private reflections live in separate places, making a relationship difficult to revisit as a whole.

The material is sensitive

Intimate records require clear controls, restrained language, and a data model designed around privacy from the start.

How it works

A Moment connects personal writing with people and context, then makes each record easy to capture, revisit, organize, and understand.

Design approach

Kindred handles sensitive material, so trust must be visible in the interface and supported by the data model. Clear structure, visible control, restrained language, and familiar system behavior keep the experience calm and understandable.

Privacy by design

These experience principles are supported by local-first storage, protected access, and practical data controls.

Private storage

SwiftData keeps records on the device and CloudKit can sync them through the user's private iCloud database. Kindred has no separate account, advertising, or third-party analytics SDK.

Protected access

Optional App Lock uses Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode, while a privacy shield covers sensitive content when the app leaves the foreground. Apple Health access and individual syncs remain optional.

Data ownership

ZIP backup and restore make records portable, while in-app deletion controls let people remove stored content.
