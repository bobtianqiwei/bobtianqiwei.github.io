Availability Sync

Share your availability across calendars. Keep personal details private.

Bob Tianqi Wei
macOS Development · SwiftUI · EventKit · 2026

Availability Sync brings busy time from your personal calendars into one work calendar so coworkers can see when you are available. It works with calendar accounts already added to macOS and lets you choose what each source calendar shares.

Download for macOS

Requires macOS 14 or later. Unzip the download, move Availability Sync.app to Applications, and grant Full Calendar Access.

How it works

Privacy

Calendar processing runs locally through EventKit. Busy events omit titles, notes, locations, attendees, and account details. The app has no account, server, analytics, or telemetry. Optional iCloud sync shares settings without event data.
